# Wedding Invite Canva Guide

This guide matches the website in `/Users/kushalaa/Documents/WeddingInvite/index.html` so the digital invite and Canva design share the same direction.

## Visual Direction

- Tone: ethereal, luxurious, editorial, romantic
- Mood words: burgundy velvet, candle glow, brushed gold, lacquered jewel box, ceremonial elegance
- Structure: generous whitespace, oversized serif headings, sculpted cards, subtle gradients, polished glass panels

## Palette

- Warm Ivory: `#F4ECE4`
- Soft Sand: `#EBDDD0`
- Burgundy: `#5E0F22`
- Rose Lacquer: `#A64654`
- Antique Gold: `#B48A4F`
- Deep Garnet: `#31161F`
- Muted Wine: `#6D4B52`

## Typography

- Heading font: Cormorant Garamond
- Body font: Manrope
- Canva fallback if unavailable:
  - Heading: Playfair Display or Libre Baskerville
  - Body: DM Sans or Montserrat

## Canva Layout Recommendations

- Page 1: Hero cover with couple names, "Wedding Weekend", and May 6 to May 8 on a burgundy and ivory field
- Page 2: Event schedule with four sculpted cards and gold dividers
- Page 3: Venue and directions with a QR code linked to Google Maps
- Page 4: Closing card with venue reminder, date recap, and a directions QR code

## Motion and Decorative Treatment

- Use slow reveal fades and upward movement on each section
- Add translucent burgundy gradients, floral overlays, or brushed gold corner light
- Keep animation restrained and luxurious, never playful
- Use rounded frames, fine borders, and light grain or paper texture
