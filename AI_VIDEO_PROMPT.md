# Opening Film Prompt

Use this to generate the intro video asset for the website opener.

## Goal

Create a short luxury editorial video that starts from the same composition as the reference image: a gloved hand holding a refined invitation envelope/card against a deep burgundy backdrop. The same hand then slowly opens the envelope in one elegant motion.

## Shot Spec

- Duration: 5 to 7 seconds
- Format: vertical composition that can also be center-cropped for desktop
- Motion: minimal, controlled, couture, premium
- Camera: locked or very subtle push-in
- Lighting: dramatic soft studio lighting, warm highlights, rich shadows
- Background: deep Cartier-style burgundy
- Styling: black lace glove, stacked gold rings, ivory textured envelope/card, refined serif or script typography
- Action: one hand holds the envelope steady, thumb lifts flap, envelope opens slowly and gracefully
- End frame: envelope open, invitation beginning to reveal

## Prompt

```text
Create a luxury editorial fashion-film shot in a deep burgundy studio setting. A feminine hand wearing a black lace glove and elegant stacked gold rings holds an ivory textured invitation envelope centered in frame. The styling feels haute couture, refined, cinematic, and expensive, similar to a luxury jewelry campaign. Keep the composition clean and minimal with rich contrast and soft directional lighting.

The shot begins with the envelope already held upright in the hand. Over 5 to 7 seconds, the same hand slowly and gracefully opens the envelope flap in one smooth motion. The movement is delicate, poised, and realistic. Maintain continuity of the same hand, same glove, same rings, same envelope, and same camera angle throughout the shot. Add subtle fabric and finger motion, but avoid dramatic camera movement. No extra hands, no sudden cuts, no morphing, no surreal distortion, no jitter.

Visual style: burgundy Cartier-like luxury campaign, cinematic macro detail, tactile paper texture, warm gold highlights, soft shadow falloff, premium editorial finish.
```

## Negative Prompt

```text
extra hands, changing jewelry, changing glove pattern, changing envelope shape, text distortion, warped fingers, flicker, jump cuts, fast motion, surreal transitions, shaky camera, plastic skin, low-detail fabric
```
